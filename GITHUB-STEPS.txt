GET THE .EXE WITHOUT INSTALLING ANYTHING (free)
1. Go to github.com and create a free account.
2. Click + (top right) > New repository. Name: shivraj-medical. Click Create repository.
3. Click "uploading an existing file". UNZIP this zip on your PC first, then drag ALL
   the files and folders inside it into the page (include the hidden ".github" folder).
   Click Commit changes.
4. Click the "Actions" tab. A build starts by itself (about 5 minutes).
5. When it shows a green tick, click it, scroll to "Artifacts" and download
   "Shivraj-Medical-Installer". Unzip it and run the .exe.
